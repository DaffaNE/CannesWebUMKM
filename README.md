# Cannes

**Websites for Indonesian small businesses. Fast, honest, ready to sell.**

Cannes is a web studio that designs and builds websites for UMKM, the micro, small, and medium enterprises that make up most of Indonesia's business landscape. The work is practical: a clean site that loads quickly on a phone, explains the business clearly, and makes it easy for customers to order or ask questions.

Every project is quoted before work starts, built mobile-first, and handed over with full ownership of the domain, hosting, and files.

## Who we work with

- Food and beverage businesses, from warung to catering
- Fashion, batik, and craft brands
- Service businesses such as salons, barbershops, workshops, and consultants
- Small shops moving from social media DMs to their own online store
- Growing businesses that need a custom system instead of a template

## What we build

**Company profile website.** A clear introduction to the business, its services, location, and contact channels, with WhatsApp built into every page.

**Online store.** A product catalogue with QRIS, e-wallet, and virtual account payments, shipping cost calculation, vouchers, and links to marketplaces.

**Landing page.** One focused page for a promotion, launch, or campaign, built to convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations.

**Custom web application.** Booking flows, dashboards, API integrations, and multi-language systems designed around how the business actually works.

**Care and maintenance.** Updates, backups, small content changes, and technical support after launch.

## How a project runs

1. **Conversation.** The client describes the business, the goal, and the deadline through WhatsApp.
2. **Scope and quote.** Cannes confirms the package, add-ons, timeline, and total price.
3. **Deposit.** A 50 percent down payment starts the project. QRIS, GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, and bank transfer are accepted.
4. **Design and build.** The site is built mobile-first, with progress shared for review.
5. **Revisions.** The agreed number of revision rounds is used to refine content and layout.
6. **Launch and handover.** The site goes live, and domain, hosting, and file access are transferred to the client.

## About this website

This site is a static build made of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no framework, no build step, and no database, so it can be hosted on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or ordinary shared hosting.

- Indonesian is the default language, with an English toggle. In `index.html`, Indonesian copy lives in the markup and English copy lives in the dictionary inside `assets/js/app.js`.
- Fonts are self-hosted and embedded in `assets/css/fonts.css`, so there are no requests to external font services.
- The contact form does not send data to a server. It validates the fields and opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.
- There are no cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are included so the three public pages can be indexed. Both currently use `https://cannes.id/` as a placeholder. Replace it with the live domain in `index.html`, `sla.html`, `credits.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` before publishing.
- Photo, font, and icon attribution is listed on `credits.html`.
